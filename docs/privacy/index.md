# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Languages: [English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文) · [繁體中文](#繁體中文) · [Español](#español) · [日本語](#日本語) · [한국어](#한국어) · [Deutsch](#deutsch) · [Français](#français) · [العربية](#العربية) · [Русский](#русский)

## English

PortfolioHub stores portfolio assets, quantities, costs, liabilities, tags, notes, settings, and snapshots on your device. When iCloud is available, this data may be mirrored to your private iCloud key-value store so it can sync across your devices. The developer does not operate a server that receives your portfolio.

When you search for an asset or refresh market data, PortfolioHub sends the necessary search term, symbol, provider identifier, and requested currency to the market-data providers you enable. These providers may receive your IP address and standard network metadata and handle them under their own privacy policies. Providers may include Yahoo Finance, Sina Finance, Binance, CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, and ExchangeRate-API.

PortfolioHub contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. StoreKit purchases are processed by Apple. Face ID is handled by iOS and biometric information is never available to PortfolioHub. Password lock stores only a salted hash on the device.

You can remove local data by deleting the app. You can remove synced data through your iCloud settings. This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. Questions can be submitted through the [support page](../support/).
