# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Languages: [English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文) · [繁體中文](#繁體中文) · [Español](#español) · [日本語](#日本語) · [한국어](#한국어) · [Deutsch](#deutsch) · [Français](#français) · [العربية](#العربية) · [Русский](#русский)

## English

WorthTrail stores portfolio assets, quantities, costs, liabilities, position-adjustment records, tags, notes, settings, and snapshots on your device using SwiftData. If you use iCloud sync, the latest portfolio backup is stored in your private CloudKit database. The developer does not operate a server that receives your portfolio.

You may export a password-protected local backup. Backup contents are encrypted on the device before export. WorthTrail widgets receive only the summary data required for display through the app's private App Group container. The privacy widget does not include portfolio amounts.

When you search for an asset or refresh market data, WorthTrail sends the necessary search term, symbol, provider identifier, and requested currency to the market-data providers you enable. These providers may receive your IP address and standard network metadata and handle them under their own privacy policies. Providers may include Yahoo Finance, Sina Finance, Binance, CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, and ExchangeRate-API.

WorthTrail contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. StoreKit purchases are processed by Apple. Face ID is handled by iOS and biometric information is never available to WorthTrail. Password lock stores only a salted hash on the device.

You can remove local data by deleting the app. You can remove synced data through your iCloud settings and delete exported backup files wherever you saved them. This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. Questions can be submitted through the [support page](../support/).
