01

Data Stored on Your Device

WorthTrail stores your assets, quantities, cost basis, liabilities, position-adjustment records, dividends, recurring plans, tags, notes, settings, and historical snapshots locally on your device using Apple SwiftData.

The developer does not operate any backend server that receives, stores, or inspects your portfolio data, and WorthTrail does not require creating a user account.

02

Optional Private iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your portfolio data syncs exclusively through your personal Apple CloudKit private database associated with your Apple Account.

The developer has no access to your private CloudKit database. Synced data is governed by Apple’s iCloud security and privacy architecture.

03

Market Data & Exchange Rate Providers

When you search for an asset or refresh prices and exchange rates, WorthTrail sends only the necessary search term, symbol, provider identifier, and requested currency to the market-data providers you have enabled under Settings > Data Sources & Status.

These providers—which may include Yahoo Finance, Webull, Sina Finance, Binance, OKX, CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, TWSE, ExchangeRate-API, and supported bank wealth product feeds—may receive your IP address and standard network metadata and process them under their own privacy policies. WorthTrail never sends your portfolio quantities, cost basis, or account balances to any market-data provider.

04

On-Device OCR, Widgets, Backups & Diagnostics

Screenshot Batch Import (OCR): Images you select for batch import are recognized locally on your device using Apple’s on-device Vision framework and are not uploaded to WorthTrail servers.

Widgets: WorthTrail Home Screen widgets read only the summary data required for display through the app’s private App Group container. The Privacy Widget omits all monetary amounts.

Local Backups & Diagnostics: Password-protected local backups are encrypted on your device before export. Optional .wtdiag diagnostic files are generated only when you initiate an export to help troubleshoot an issue and are encrypted before leaving the app.

05

Mac Local MCP Server

WorthTrail for Mac includes an optional local Streamable HTTP MCP server bound strictly to 127.0.0.1 (localhost) and authenticated with a Bearer token stored in your macOS Keychain. It is disabled by default, read-only unless you separately enable write access, and requires a two-step preview and confirmation code for every write.

If you connect WorthTrail’s local MCP server to an AI client (such as Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or Codex), any portfolio data you instruct that AI client to query will be handled according to that AI client’s and model provider’s own privacy policy.

06

No Ads, No Tracking, Purchases & App Lock

WorthTrail contains no advertising, no cross-app tracking, and no third-party analytics SDKs.

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed exclusively by Apple StoreKit. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS/macOS, and biometric data is never accessible to WorthTrail. Passcode lock stores only a salted cryptographic hash on your device.

07

Deleting Your Data, Policy Updates & Contact

You can remove all local data at any time by deleting WorthTrail from your device. You can manage or remove iCloud-synced data through your device’s Apple iCloud settings, and delete any exported backup, Excel, PDF, or diagnostic files wherever you saved them.

This privacy policy may be updated when app capabilities change. If you have any privacy questions, please reach out via the Support page or email support@worthtrail.app.
