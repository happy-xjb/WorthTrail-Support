Facts & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about WorthTrail’s privacy model, asset coverage, pricing, and Mac MCP server.

Does WorthTrail require connecting a bank or brokerage account?

No. WorthTrail is intentionally built without bank login or Plaid. You record your positions, cash, deposits, and liabilities directly, while public market quotes and foreign exchange rates update automatically.

Where is my portfolio data stored?

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple SwiftData. Optional multi-device sync uses your personal Apple iCloud (CloudKit) private database. The developer does not run a server that collects or sees your portfolio, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics SDKs.

How does pricing work? Is there a one-time purchase?

Yes. The free tier includes all features for up to 10 items (assets, cash accounts, and liabilities combined). WorthTrail Pro removes the 10-item cap only: US$0.99/month, US$5.99/year, or US$12.99 one-time Lifetime (equalized across 175 App Store storefronts, e.g., ¥8/month, ¥38/year, ¥88 Lifetime in mainland China).

What asset types and markets are supported?

WorthTrail tracks global stocks (US, HK, CN A-shares, TW, and international exchanges), ETFs, mutual funds, cryptocurrencies, options, fixed-term deposits (CDs, GICs, term deposits), supported bank wealth products, cash, custom manually valued assets (real estate, gold, private equity, bonds), and liabilities (mortgages, loans, credit cards). All currencies convert into your chosen base currency.

Which market data providers does WorthTrail use?

You can enable, disable, and reorder data sources in Settings > Data Sources & Status, including Yahoo Finance, Webull, Binance, OKX, Sina Finance, TWSE, ExchangeRate-API, and supported bank wealth product feeds. Only the necessary symbol and currency queries are sent to the providers you enable.

Does WorthTrail support dividends, FIRE, and rebalancing?

Yes. WorthTrail includes a dividend calendar with ex-date booking and tax-adjusted passive income, dividend growth and concentration analytics, a customizable FIRE dashboard (target, passive income coverage, projected date, and plan-to-age sustainability), recurring investment (DCA) plans, stock split adjustments, and a target allocation rebalancing calculator.

How does the Mac local MCP server work?

On Mac, WorthTrail includes an optional local Streamable HTTP MCP server (http://127.0.0.1:47821/mcp) secured with a Keychain Bearer token. You can connect Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or Codex to query net worth, holdings, returns, dividends, and FIRE metrics. Write actions are disabled by default and always require a two-step preview and confirmation code. See the Mac MCP guide.

Can I export my data or back it up locally?

Yes. You can export password-encrypted local backups (and restore them anytime), as well as Excel (.xlsx) workbooks and PDF reports for archiving or external analysis.

Which platforms are supported?

WorthTrail is available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac via the Apple App Store. It is localized in 45+ languages. Android and web versions are not currently available.
