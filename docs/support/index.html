In-App Support & Encrypted Diagnostics

Inside WorthTrail, open Settings > Contact & Support to send an email directly or export an Encrypted Diagnostics File (.wtdiag) when troubleshooting tricky data-source or calculation issues.

Privacy reminder: Please do not include account numbers, passwords, API keys, or sensitive personal financial documents in plain support emails. If you share a diagnostics file, only send it when needed for an active issue.

Quick Troubleshooting

Most common questions can be resolved directly in Settings without waiting for a reply.

Market Data, Quotes & Custom Assets
  • Check quote connectivity under Settings > Data Sources & Status. The status test calls the exact same endpoint as live refreshes.
  • Some providers (such as Yahoo Finance) may be rate-limited or unreachable on certain networks; you can reorder or switch providers anytime.
  • For unlisted holdings, real estate, gold, or private funds, add a Custom Asset and update its valuation whenever you wish.
Holdings, Cost Basis, Splits & Batch Import
  • Record buys, sells, or position adjustments with an optional execution price and date so historical quantities and cost basis stay accurate.
  • Use Batch Import to import multiple holdings from CSV files or broker screenshots (OCR runs entirely on-device).
  • Use Stock Split on an asset to adjust historical quantities and cost basis cleanly after a split or reverse split.
  • Historical snapshots populate your Net Worth curve and P&L Calendar and are included in both iCloud sync and encrypted local backups.
iCloud Sync, Encrypted Backups & Pro Restore
  • Optional multi-device sync runs through your personal Apple iCloud (CloudKit) private database—ensure iCloud Drive/Sync is signed in with the same Apple Account on all devices.
  • You can export and restore password-encrypted local backup files anytime in Settings.
  • To restore a Pro subscription or Lifetime purchase on a new device, open the Pro screen in Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
  • WorthTrail has no user account: deleting the app removes local data, and iCloud data can be managed directly in Apple iCloud settings.
Privacy Lock, Widgets & Mac MCP
  • Tap the eye icon next to Net Worth to hide monetary amounts on screen.
  • Add WorthTrail from the iOS Home Screen widget gallery; the privacy widget shows only percentage change and a trend line.
  • For Mac MCP connection issues, confirm WorthTrail is open on Mac, Settings > Agent Access is enabled, the app is unlocked, and your client uses http://127.0.0.1:47821/mcp with your current Keychain Bearer token.
